# Personal Portfolio

Hey, I'm **Jesse Kimsupasuk**, an Electrical and Electronics Engineering (Hons) student at the University of Canterbury. This is a personal repository of everything I have built to showcase my projects over the years! Later this year, I'll create a portfolio website to make it easier to navigate and find the projects that interest you the most.

This is a place where I go into more detail about the projects I've completed both inside and outside of my courses, so any questions about them are always appreciated. A summary of the projects included is shown below:

* **Australian Universities Rocketry Competition (Kōtare) 2026** - Photos and videos from the manufacturing process are compiled into one place. They mostly cover the build process, CAD modelling, composite manufacturing, and avionics.
* **Solar-powered Car** - A power electronics project using first principles calculations and simulations using LTspice to first verify that the design will work. Achieved goals to power a mechanical car by designing a DC-DC buck converter, which takes a solar panel voltage input and stepping it down to the maximum power point of the panel for maximum torque drive.
* **Tempo Rhythm Device** - An electronic project taken from schematic capture to protoboard design used to improve tempo of new drummers. Achieved goals of measuring accurate hits to output the accuracy of the beat you were tapping at.
* **Serial-programmable Robot** - A project taken through schematic capture, PCB layouts, and CAD modelling used to draw programmable shapes through serial inputs.
* **Passion Projects** - These consist of various projects that are currently underway. At the moment, I am developing a solar-powered drone alongside my studies, with plans to develop a CubeSat payload under Low-earth Orbit conditions per the NZUSC Challenge.
* **AURC & High Powered Rocketry Documentation** - A write-up of engineering problems I faced during AURC 2026, and how I solved them.
